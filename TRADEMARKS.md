# The BifluxVR name and icon

BifluxVR is free software, and you're welcome to fork it under the
[license](LICENSE). The name and icon are treated differently from the code,
so people can tell the original from a fork.

**You can:**

- Say that your project is "based on BifluxVR" or "a fork of BifluxVR".
- Write about BifluxVR, review it, or link to it.
- Package unmodified releases for a Linux distribution under the BifluxVR name.

**You can't, without asking first:**

- Release a modified version named BifluxVR or a similar name, such as
  "BifluxVR Pro".
- Use the BifluxVR icon, or a close copy of it, for your own project.
- Suggest that intramy made or endorses your version.

A fork needs its own name and icon, and must credit the original as described
in [NOTICE](NOTICE). To ask about anything else, open an issue or find us on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/U9XQ9eeGkx).

The Tux part of the icon is Larry Ewing's and isn't claimed here. Tux stays
free for anyone to use under his terms.
